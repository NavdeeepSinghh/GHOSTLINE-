Climbing: only yellow-edged surfaces can be climbed. Jump toward a ledge to grab it,
hold forward or press Jump to pull up, move sideways to shimmy, Crouch to drop,
hold back and press Jump to hop off.

Stealth: crouching is quiet and harder to see. Shadow shortens enemy sight; crouching in tall
grass hides you almost completely. An eye marker above an enemy fills as it notices you.
Press Interact behind an unaware enemy for a silent takedown (not on armoured enemies).

Keyboard, mouse and controller buttons can be changed under Settings, Controls (sticks and triggers are fixed). Double-tap a direction to dodge (can be turned off). Block can be set to toggle instead of hold. The Access tab has subtitle, colour and flashing options. Rest at a workbench (Interact) to save, refill and upgrade weapons.

Bosses: a bar at the top shows the boss's health. The Warden's flame cannot pass the yard's pillars.
The Choir can only be hurt at its glowing heart while the heart is swollen and bright; jump its
shockwave rings, and in the last phase follow Lyra's calls ("Left!", "Right!", "Jump!").
Facility cameras (Sentinels) show the cone they watch. Stay out of it, or switch them off at a security desk.

Stuck? Press **T** (controller: D-pad down) for a hint about the current objective. The objective marker swells so it is easy to find. If you stay on the same objective for a while, hints also appear on their own.

Main menu > **Missions** starts any part of the game directly. Progress made in a mission is not saved.

Facility cameras: each has a yellow cable box on the wall beneath it. Sneak under the cone and press E to cut it.

## Photo mode
Pause > **Photo mode**. The world freezes and a free camera flies around Kael (up to 14 m away, never through walls).

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Look | Mouse | Right stick |
| Move / up / down | WASD, Space, Ctrl (Shift faster) | Left stick, D-pad up / down |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel | Triggers |
| Roll | Q / E | Bumpers |
| Filter (None, Noir, Dusk, Cold, Echo, Faded film) | F | Y |
| Widescreen bars | L | |
| Depth of field, focus | B, [ and ] | |
| Hide Kael | K | X |
| Hide the panel | H | Back / View |
| Reset camera | R | |
| Save photo | Enter, P or left click | A |
| Back to the pause menu | Esc | B / Start |

Photos are saved as PNG files in the game's data folder, under `photos` (on a Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/Ghostline/photos`).

## New Game+
Finish the game once and the main menu shows **New Game+**: start again from the rooftop with your weapons, their upgrades and your salvage. Enemies are tougher each time round (up to the third cycle).
